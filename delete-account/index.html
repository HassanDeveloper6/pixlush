Deleting your PixLush account

Last updated 1 September 2026

You can delete your PixLush account and everything stored with it. Here is what goes, what stays, and how to do it.

From inside the app

Open PixLush and go to the You tab, then Account, then Delete account. You will be asked for your password — that is Google Firebase confirming it is really you, and it is the only thing standing between someone holding your unlocked phone and your account.

If you no longer have the app

Email the support address on our App Store or Google Play listing from the address the account uses, and ask for it to be deleted. We will confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

Your sign-in and your account record — the email address, the count of AI edits used today, and whether a subscription is active. All of it, permanently, with nothing kept in a backup for later.

What is not

Photos and edits saved on your phone stay on your phone. They were never ours to delete.

A subscription is NOT cancelled by deleting your account. It lives with Apple or Google and only they can cancel it — do that first in the App Store or Play Store, or you will keep being charged for an account that no longer exists.
