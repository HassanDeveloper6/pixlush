Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

PixLush is a photo editor. Most of what it does happens on your phone and involves nobody else. This explains the parts that do not.

Your photos

Filters, brightness and colour, crop, backgrounds, stickers, text and frames are all applied on your phone. Those photos are never uploaded, and the editor works with no connection at all.

The AI features are the exception, and they are the only exception. They are described in full in the next section.

AI edits and Google

What is sent. When you use an AI feature — Just Ask, Erase or Replace, AI Upscale, or the one-tap AI presets in the editor — PixLush sends the photo you chose for that edit together with the words you typed. For Erase or Replace it also sends a short description of where on the photo you painted. Create an image sends only the words. Nothing else is sent: not your other photos, your contacts or your location.

How it is collected. Only when you start an AI edit yourself, by tapping it. Nothing is sent in the background, and nothing is sent by any other part of the app.

Who receives it. Google. The edit is made by Google Gemini, which PixLush reaches through Firebase AI Logic, a Google service. The request is made through your PixLush session, so that your AI edits can be counted.

What it is used for. To make the edit you asked for and return it to your phone, and for nothing else. It is not used for advertising, it is not used to build a profile of you, and Google does not use it to train its AI models. We do not keep a copy of your photo or of the result on any server.

Your permission. PixLush asks before the first AI edit and sends nothing until you allow it. You can withdraw that permission at any time under You › AI data sharing; the AI features will then ask again before anything is sent. The rest of the app works without it.

How it is protected. Your photo travels encrypted. Google processes it on our behalf under its data processing terms, which commit it to protecting your data to the same standard as this policy.

Your account

An account is optional. It exists so a Pro subscription follows you to a new phone and so your daily free edits are counted somewhere you cannot edit.

If you create one we store your email address, and against it two things: how many AI edits you have used today, and whether your subscription is active. That is the whole record. Your password is handled by Google Firebase and is never visible to us.

Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through the App Store or Google Play. Card details, billing addresses and payment history stay with them — they never reach us. All we are told is whether a subscription is active.

What we do not do

There is no analytics in this app. No crash reporting, no advertising SDK, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no profile built from what you edit. We do not sell or share personal information.

Others who process data for us

Google Firebase provides sign-in and the small account record described above. Google Gemini, reached through Firebase, produces the AI edits, as set out under “AI edits and Google”. Each of them handles your data only to provide that service, under terms that require the same protection this policy gives it. Apple and Google handle payments. Sticker artwork is fetched from Iconify when you open the sticker picker; that request carries no photo and no account information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: You → Account → Delete account. It removes your sign-in and the account record, permanently and immediately.

Two things it does not touch, both on purpose. Photos and edits saved on your phone stay on your phone. And a subscription is not cancelled by deleting an account — it lives with Apple or Google, and only they can cancel it, so do that first or you will keep being charged.

Children

PixLush is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update it here and in the app, and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about any of this, or about data we hold: get in touch through the support address on our App Store or Google Play listing.
